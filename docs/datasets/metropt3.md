## Inconsistencies in the description PDF
- It states 15,169,480 rows at 1 Hz. The file has 1,516,948 rows at 10 s; the UCI page says 0.1 Hz. The PDF figures appear to describe the original logging.
- The failure table numbers two events "#1". They are numbered 1 to 4 in order here.
- Event 2 lists "Maintenance on 30Apr at 12:00", but its failure window is 29 to 30 May. The maintenance date is stored as published with a caveat and is not used for labels.
- The paper DOI in the master doc (`...9564201`) is a typo; `10.1109/DSAA53316.2021.9564181` resolves to the paper.
- `DV_eletric` is misspelt in the source; the name is kept.

## Label semantics
Labels come from `configs/metropt3_failure_windows.yaml`:
- `documented_failure_window`: inside a reported window (bounds inclusive).
- `pre_event_window`: within the configurable horizon (default 24 h) before a window start. The horizon is an assumption, not a fact.
- `unlabelled_or_unknown`: everything else. These rows are **not** known to be healthy.

Window timestamps are approximate and their timezone is unknown; they are treated as local naive time, like the CSV.

## Processing
Segments split at gaps greater than 60 s with no interpolation (see ADR-0002). Processed output goes to `datasets/processed/metropt3.parquet` (gitignored) with `segment_id` and `label` columns.

## Prohibited claims
- Do not call unlabelled rows "healthy" or "normal operation".
- Do not claim a defect is confirmed, or that a failure was predicted or prevented. Results are "anomaly detected" at most.
- The four events are all air leaks on one compressor. Nothing here generalises to other failure modes, other units or other fleets.
- ML output never overrides the deterministic rule engine.
